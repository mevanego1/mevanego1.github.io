---
title: "Inventory"
subtitle: "A very short story"
date: 2026-08-15
genre: Flash Fiction
summary: "A list of what was left in the apartment, and what it says about who left it."
---

One coffee mug, chipped, reading WORLD'S OKAYEST UNCLE.

Two keys on a ring shaped like a fish. Neither fits this door.

A paperback with every third page dog-eared, as if the reader kept meaning to come back and never quite did.

A houseplant, alive. Somebody had been watering it right up until the end.

A calendar still turned to March, with one date circled twice and no explanation.

I write it all down, the way they ask me to. Then I water the plant, because somebody should.
